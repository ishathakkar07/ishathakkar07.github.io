Before you browse

A short list goes a long way.

Choose a budget, name a piece you need, and leave room for one unexpected find. The best bargain is something you'll use.

Choose by what you need

Start with the category.
Then follow your eye.

If you're exploring secondhand stores for clothing, begin with everyday styles you know you'll wear. A basic tee or comfortable pair of trousers can be easier to work into your wardrobe than an unfamiliar statement piece.

For vintage inspiration, the vintage clothing guide focuses on mixing textures and proportions. For a first thrift trip, the beginner tips explain what to inspect before you take a piece home.
